Audit checklist

What to inspect

  • Page titles contain a clear topic and target keyword.
  • Meta descriptions summarize the page and encourage relevant clicks.
  • H1 and H2 headings describe the content hierarchy.
  • URLs are short, readable and topic-focused.
  • Images use descriptive alternative text when real images are added.
  • Internal links connect related articles and the keyword report.
  • Mobile layout works across common screen sizes.
