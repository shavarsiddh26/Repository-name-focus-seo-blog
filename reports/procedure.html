SEO procedure.

The exact workflow used for the practical. Add screenshots at each relevant stage when preparing your final record.

01

Choose the topic

Select a blog topic and define the audience and search intent.

02

Research keywords

Open Google Keyword Planner or Ubersuggest and search the topic.

03

Evaluate terms

Compare search volume, competition and relevance. Select one primary keyword and supporting terms.

04

Optimize content

Apply keywords naturally to title, meta description, H1/H2 headings, URL and body content.

05

Technical SEO

Add sitemap.xml, robots.txt, responsive layout, descriptive links and proper HTML structure.

06

Deploy & measure

Deploy to Vercel, submit the sitemap to Search Console and record the results.
