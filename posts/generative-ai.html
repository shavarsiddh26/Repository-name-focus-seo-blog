What Is Generative AI?

A beginner-friendly introduction to generative AI, how it works and how students can use it.

AI

What does generative AI mean?

Generative AI refers to systems that can create new content such as text, images, audio or code from learned patterns.

How does it work?

Modern systems learn statistical patterns from large datasets and generate outputs based on an input called a prompt or request.

How can students use it?

Use AI to explore ideas, explain difficult concepts, prototype code and review your work. Always verify important facts and understand the final result.
